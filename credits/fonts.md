# Font credits

Both fonts are self-hosted from `public/assets/fonts/` and declared with `@font-face` at the top of `src/ui/hud.css`. `index.html` preloads them. The HUD and the menus (`src/ui/menus.css`) share them.

## Lilita One

- **File:** `public/assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.woff2` (10,676 bytes)
- **Designer:** Juan Montoreano
- **Source:** Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lilita+One
- **License:** SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. https://openfontlicense.org
- **Used for:** display text such as the logo, headings, buttons, numbers and keycaps (`--br-display`).

## Nunito

- **File:** `public/assets/fonts/Nunito-Variable.woff2` (39,152 bytes, variable weight axis 200–1000)
- **Designer:** Vernon Adams, later expanded by Cyreal and Jacques Le Bailly
- **Source:** Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito
- **License:** SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. https://openfontlicense.org
- **Used for:** body and UI text (`--br-body`).
